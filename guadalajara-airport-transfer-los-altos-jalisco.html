Straight home from the airport

Flying in from the U.S. to see family, especially around December? Skip the bus connections and the taxi haggling. We meet you at arrivals with a sign with your name, load your bags and drive you to your family's door in Los Altos.

Local families use the same service to pick up or drop off relatives at Guadalajara Airport. And if you are heading to Los Altos to visit its tequila distilleries, we can take you from the airport straight to your town. One private vehicle, one price per zone, no shared rides.

How to book

  1. Message us on WhatsApp with your flight number, date, number of passengers and the town you are going to.
  2. We confirm the price for your zone and any surcharge that applies.
  3. Secure your ride with a $500 MXN deposit, which is deducted from the total.
  4. On arrival day we wait for you at the exit with your sign.
