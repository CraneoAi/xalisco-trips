The ride

Guadalajara International Airport to Arandas is almost 2 hours by highway. We track your flight, meet you at the arrivals exit with a sign, help with your bags and drop you at the address you give us.

Landing between 10:00 pm and 6:00 am adds a night surcharge of $500 MXN.

Same fare nearby

The Arandas fare also covers door-to-door drop-off in:

  • Jesús María
  • Atotonilco
  • Ayotlán

Going to another town in Los Altos? See the prices by zone.

Arandas back to the airport

For your flight home we pick you up at your door in Arandas and drive you to Guadalajara Airport. Tell us your departure time when you book and we will reserve both rides at once. The return ride has the same zone rate as the arrival trip, with the same night surcharge if it applies.

Rides to Arandas tequila distilleries

Visiting Los Altos for its tequila? We drive you to distilleries in the Arandas area, such as El Pandillo or Tequila Ocho, and wait while you do your visit.

You book the distillery visit or tour directly with the distillery; we take care of the ride and the waiting time.
